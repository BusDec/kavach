The first 72 hours.

A one-page command checklist for the CIO, CISO, plant head, general counsel and board secretary. Print it. Agree the names before you need it.

0–60 minutes · Stabilise and preserve

  • Declare an incident and name the incident commander
  • Open a single timeline and evidence log
  • Preserve logs, images and session records before cleanup
  • Isolate affected IT segments; do not power off OT blindly
  • Disable compromised accounts and revoke sessions
  • Engage legal, communications and the business owner
  • Contact the insurer and follow the notification clause
  • Brief the CEO and board chair; agree the next update time
  • Do not pay, negotiate or publicise without a decision
  • Record every external communication and decision
  • Check safety, environmental and regulatory exposure
  • Start the vendor and third-party contact list

OT-specific: isolate through the network and access layer, not by switching off controllers. Confirm safety-system status with the plant head before any containment action. Manual operation is a business decision, not an IT decision.

The board script · five questions

  • What is the operational impact right now?
  • What data and how many people are affected?
  • Which regulators and contracts are triggered?
  • Who is in charge, and what do they need?
  • What are we telling customers, staff and the market — and when?

01 · 1–24 hours · Scope and decide

Turn the incident into decisions.

Technical

  • Confirm initial access and persistence
  • Identify data accessed, copied or encrypted
  • Check backups and restore capability
  • Hunt the same indicator across IT and OT
  • Preserve evidence for counsel and insurer
  • Agree the recovery sequence with operations

Legal and regulatory

  • Assess GDPR 72-hour, CERT-In 6-hour and SEC 4-day clocks
  • Decide who notifies, in what order and with what words
  • Check sector regulators, contracts and insurers
  • Privilege the investigation where available
  • Prepare the first holding statement
  • Record the materiality judgement and who made it

24–72 hours · Notify, recover, learn

  • Complete the regulator notification decision
  • Notify affected individuals if required
  • Brief the board with facts, not speculation
  • Set a cadence for customer and staff updates
  • Approve the recovery plan and rollback criteria
  • Protect the evidence chain for the post-incident review
  • Track every action to an owner and a time
  • Watch for secondary extortion or copycat activity
  • Do not close the incident at restoration
  • Schedule the post-incident review within 14 days
  • Convert findings into funded remediation
  • Update the playbook and the board report
