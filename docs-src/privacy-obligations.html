01 · The comparison

Three regimes, three clocks.

The same incident can trigger different obligations depending on where the data subjects, the controller, the processor and the affected systems are located. Do not assume the strictest regime covers the others.

JurisdictionMain frameworkBreach clockMaximum penaltyWhat it means for SAP / OT data
European Union GDPR (2016/679); NIS2 (2022/2555) for essential and important entities; DORA for financial entities 72 hours to the supervisory authority after awareness; NIS2 early warning in 24 hours and incident notification in 72 hours GDPR: €20m or 4% of global annual turnover. NIS2: at least €10m or 2% for essential entities Employee and customer data across SAP, cloud and suppliers. OT incidents may also be NIS2 incidents. International transfers and processor clauses matter.
United States No federal omnibus privacy law; state laws (CCPA/CPRA and others), sector rules (HIPAA, GLBA), SEC disclosure SEC: material incidents on Form 8-K within four business days of the materiality determination; state breach laws vary CCPA/CPRA: $2,500 per violation, $7,500 per intentional violation or involving minors; HIPAA up to $1.5m per year per category Build a 50-state notification matrix. SEC materiality is a board judgement. Vendor contracts and data maps are evidence.
India Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and DPDP Rules, 2025; CERT-In directions; RBI, SEBI and telecom rules CERT-In: report incidents within 6 hours; DPDP notification to the Data Protection Board and affected persons without delay, as the rules phase in Up to ₹250 crore for failure to take reasonable security safeguards under the DPDP Act Consent, purpose limitation and security safeguards apply to digital personal data in SAP HR, payroll, customer and vendor records. Full obligations phase in through 2027.

The practical point: the notification clock starts when you become aware, not when you finish the investigation. The ability to assess scope in hours depends on the data map, logging and access evidence you built before the incident.

Sector overlays that change the clock

Financial services

DORA applies to EU financial entities with ICT incident reporting obligations. RBI and SEBI add Indian incident timelines. A single incident may require multiple notifications.

Critical infrastructure

NIS2 and national implementations add early warnings and sector regulators. OT incidents may be reportable even when no personal data is involved.

Telecom

Telecom security rules add sector-specific reporting and supply-chain expectations. Vendor and network data may be in scope.

Public companies

SEC materiality is a board judgement made with counsel. The 8-K clock is not the same as the privacy notification clock.

Do not treat “no personal data” as “no obligation”. An OT outage can trigger NIS2, sector reporting, contractual notification and safety duties even when no employee or customer record was touched.

03 · The readiness checklist

What to fix before the clock starts.

A short, practical checklist for the CIO, CISO, DPO and general counsel. Each item should have an owner, an evidence link and a date.

Know the data

  • Data map across SAP, HR, payroll, cloud and suppliers
  • Retention schedule and deletion evidence
  • Cross-border transfer inventory
  • Processor and vendor contract clauses
  • Records of processing and lawful basis

Know the people

  • Named incident commander and alternates
  • Legal, communications and regulator contacts
  • Board secretary and disclosure owner
  • Vendor escalation contacts

Know the clock

  • Jurisdiction matrix with notification tests
  • 72-hour / 6-hour / 4-day playbook
  • Pre-drafted notification templates
  • Evidence preservation and chain of custody
  • Tabletop completed in the last 12 months

Know the evidence

  • Access logs and privileged session records
  • Data export and exfiltration telemetry
  • Backup and restore test results
  • Control metrics reported to the board
