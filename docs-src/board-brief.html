01 · The decision

Three asks, one accountable owner.

The board is not being asked to approve a technology shopping list. It is being asked to fund a measurable reduction in the probability and duration of a business outage, a fraud event or a reportable data breach.

Ask 01

Approve a focused pilot

8–12 weeks, one business unit or one critical estate. A fixed scope with success measures agreed before kick-off.

Ask 02

Name the executive sponsor

One owner with the authority to connect CIO, CISO, plant operations, finance, legal and risk. Security without an owner is a hobby.

Ask 03

Set the board metrics

Critical SAP findings closed, mean time to patch, OT assets monitored, restore time, vendor access reviewed, privacy actions closed.

02 · The converged attack surface

SAP and the plant are one estate now.

The ERP is no longer a back-office system behind a firewall. It exchanges data with suppliers, banks, HR, cloud platforms and the plant. The identities that can reach SAP are often the same identities that can reach OT.

01 · The system of record

Protect the ERP

Payables, payroll, procurement, customers and maintenance history. A compromised SAP account can change a bank mandate, create a ghost vendor, release a payment run or close a period with bad data.

Control: SoD and critical authorisations, privileged access, SAP note and patch discipline, change control.

02 · The operating asset

Protect the plant

OT availability is a physical outcome. Ransomware or a malicious remote session can stop treatment, generation, dispatch or production. Recovery is a safety and contractual event, not a restore button.

Control: asset inventory, segmentation, remote-access control, backup and restore rehearsal, OT monitoring.

03 · The licence to operate

Protect the data

Employee, customer, contractor and payroll data sit across SAP, HR, cloud and suppliers. India, Europe and the US expect notification, evidence and accountability on a clock.

Control: data map, minimisation and retention, consent and DSAR handling, vendor clauses, incident playbook.

The path that matters

Credential → VPN or remote support → IT network → SAP or OT engineering workstation → plant network. Every step is a control point. Most incidents do not need a novel exploit; they need one unmanaged identity.

The board question is not “are we breached?” It is “which identities, vendors and networks can reach the systems that stop the business, and what evidence do we have that the path is controlled?”

04 · The cost of waiting

Fund the control, not the consequence.

The loss is rarely the ransom. It is the outage, the notification clock, the customer churn, the auditor’s questions and the management time that disappears for a quarter.

The arithmetic is deliberately simple. IBM puts the average cost of a data breach in India at ₹25.5 crore in 2026. If a programme reduces the likelihood of one such event by 10%, that is ₹2.55 crore of expected loss avoided — before OT downtime, fines, churn and management time. A focused pilot is measured in weeks; a material incident is measured in quarters.

What the board should ask

  • Which SAP and OT systems can stop the business?
  • Who can reach them from the internet or a vendor network?
  • When did we last restore a backup under test?
  • What would we tell a regulator in the first 72 hours?

What we recommend

  • Approve a focused pilot, not a blanket programme.
  • Fund the quick wins that reduce reachable paths.
  • Test recovery and notification before the incident.
  • Report control metrics quarterly, not anecdotes.

The 90-day plan

01

Weeks 1–2 · Discover

Crown jewels, SAP and OT estate, identities, vendors, internet exposure, incident history.

02

Weeks 2–4 · Assess

Control gap matrix across SAP access, SoD, patch, remote access, segmentation, backup and privacy.

03

Weeks 4–6 · Prove

Two or three quick wins: a critical SAP path, a remote-access path and a recovery test.

04

Weeks 6–8 · Rehearse

Tabletop with IT, OT, legal, communications and the business. Decision rights and notification clocks.

05

Weeks 8–12 · Decide

Costed roadmap, board brief, control KPIs and a clear ask for the next phase.
