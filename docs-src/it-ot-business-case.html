01 · The convergence

The boundary moved. The controls did not.

Historians, engineering workstations, remote support, vendor VPNs and cloud dashboards now connect the plant to the enterprise. Each connection is a business benefit and an attack path. The risk is not that IT and OT are connected; it is that they are connected without the same identity, patch and monitoring discipline.

Path 01

Credential → remote access → plant

A valid VPN, RDP or remote-support credential is enough to reach an engineering workstation. Attackers rarely need a zero-day; they need one account without MFA and one flat network behind it.

Path 02

Vendor → IT → OT

Maintenance providers, integrators and OEMs often hold privileged access across sites. A supplier breach can become your outage, as recent third-party incidents show.

Path 03

Internet-facing app → keys → lateral movement

A public dashboard, portal or web dispatcher can expose sessions, keys or credentials that reach internal servers. Fugaku’s 2026 breach started with a public status page.

What changes the probability

  • Inventory every IT/OT asset and its owner
  • Remove internet exposure that does not need to be public
  • MFA and least privilege for every remote path
  • Patch SAP and OT with a risk-based, tested cadence
  • Log and review privileged vendor sessions

What changes the duration

  • Segmentation that contains lateral movement
  • Offline, immutable backups and a tested restore
  • Decision rights before the incident
  • One incident playbook across IT, OT and legal
  • Pre-agreed communications and regulatory clocks

02 · The controls

Control the path, not just the device.

Most OT security programmes start with a tool. Start with the paths that can stop the business, then choose the control that closes or monitors each path.

ControlWhat good looks likeEvidence the board can see
Asset inventoryEvery SAP client, plant, PLC, HMI, historian, gateway and remote path has an owner, version and exposure status.Coverage %; unknown assets; owner sign-off
SegmentationIT, DMZ, OT and safety zones with deny-by-default flows and documented exceptions. No flat plant network.Zone map; open flows; exceptions approved
Remote accessMFA, just-in-time access, session recording and a named approver for vendors and engineers.Privileged sessions reviewed; standing access removed
Patch and configurationRisk-based cadence for SAP and OT; compensating controls where patching is unsafe; tested rollback.Mean time to patch; open criticals; compensating controls
MonitoringIdentity, network and OT telemetry correlated; alerts tied to an owner and a runbook.Mean time to detect; alerts closed; false-positive rate
RecoveryOffline backups, a tested restore sequence and a manual-operation plan with safety sign-off.Restore time; last test date; gaps closed
Incident responseOne playbook for IT, OT, legal, communications and the board; tested in a tabletop.Tabletop completed; actions closed; notification test

The trap: treating OT like IT. You cannot reboot a turbine or a treatment train on a Tuesday patch window. The answer is not to skip the control; it is to design a compensating control, a safe maintenance window and a tested rollback.

03 · The business case

What the board is buying.

A lower probability of a material incident, a shorter duration when one happens, and evidence that the organisation can answer its regulator, insurer and customers.

Without the programme

  • Unknown remote paths and vendor accounts
  • SAP criticals open beyond the patch window
  • Backups never restored under test
  • Incident roles invented during the incident
  • Regulator questions answered from memory

With the programme

  • Every path has an owner, a control and a metric
  • Critical SAP and OT exposures closed or compensated
  • Recovery time known and rehearsed
  • Decision rights and notification clocks pre-agreed
  • One evidence pack for board, auditor and insurer

The pilot. 8–12 weeks, one business unit or one critical estate. We map the paths, test two or three controls, rehearse the incident and leave a costed 90-day plan. Success measures are agreed before kick-off.
