Why Kavach

Armour, not alarm.

Kavach is the Sanskrit word for armour. The work is not a dashboard of red dots; it is the control layer that keeps the enterprise standing when an identity, a vendor or a plant network is tested.

We start with the systems that matter, map the paths an attacker would take, and leave the client with controls, owners and evidence — not a slide deck.
