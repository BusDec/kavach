Privacy notice

Your details, and what we do with them.

Last updated 18 November 2026 · Nirmana ERP, Bhubaneswar, India

This notice explains how the Kavach website handles the information you submit through the pilot request. It is a short, practical notice for a business-to-business website. It is not legal advice and does not replace the privacy notice of any contract you sign with us.

What we collect

The pilot form asks for your name, work email, phone number, company, role, sector, geography, estate in scope, preferred start, the number of users or sites in scope, and the outcome you want to improve. The form is a static page: it does not store your details on this website. Submitting it opens your email client with the request addressed to pilot@nirmanaerp.com.

Why we use it

  • To respond to your pilot request and assess whether the pilot is a fit.
  • To prepare for a conversation about scope, timing and the people involved.
  • To keep a record of the request for follow-up, if you ask us to.

Lawful basis

We rely on your consent when you tick the box and submit the request, and on our legitimate interest in responding to a business enquiry. You can withdraw consent at any time by writing to pilot@nirmanaerp.com.

What we do not do

We do not sell your details. We do not use the pilot form to send marketing without a separate opt-in. We do not ask for passwords, production data, personal data of your employees or confidential incident evidence at this stage.

Sharing

Your request may be seen by the Nirmana ERP team members who need to respond. We use service providers for email and hosting; they process the information on our instructions. If a conversation moves to a contract, the contract’s data protection terms apply.

Retention

We keep a pilot enquiry for up to 12 months after the last contact, unless a contract or a legal obligation requires a longer period. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your information, to object to processing, or to receive a copy. Write to pilot@nirmanaerp.com and we will respond within the time required by applicable law. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Cookies and analytics

This site is a static export. It does not set advertising cookies or run a third-party analytics tag. Your hosting provider may keep standard server logs for security and availability.
